# Nimbus FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you.

## Do you offer a free trial?
Yes - every new account includes a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

## How do I get a refund?
Full refunds are available within 14 days of purchase, and duplicate charges
are always refunded in full. See the refund policy for details.

## Can I change my plan later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings then Billing.
